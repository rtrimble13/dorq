# DQ203 unreported-split

**Family:** price action · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV
bars · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(an error or a missing split)) ·
**Classification:** context gap

## What it reports

The raw price changes level by a split ratio, share volume moves inversely, the
new level holds, and the data records no split. The prices are probably right;
what is missing is the split that explains them.

```
LIQ03  2022-01-07  DQ203 error  unreported-split  close 199.99→67 (×0.335 ≈ a 3:1 split), volume ×3.087 after; P(error) = >0.99
      → add split 3:1 ex 2022-01-07
      hypotheses: unreported_split >0.999 market_move <0.001 scale_error <0.001 bad_print <0.001 bad_close <0.001
      evidence (log Bayes factor against market_move):
        prior 0.02  -3.8
        return 0.335 (nearest split 3:1)  +12.6
        next_bars 1.018 (the largest of the next 5 moves)  +0.7
        open_high_low 0.3337  +0.7
        volume_on_day 4.465 (against the median before)  +0.1
        volume_shift 3.087 (median after / before)  +1.1
```

## The model

The `unreported_split` hypothesis of the price model (doc/checks/DQ201.md): the
move is a split's price factor plus an ordinary return, and the bars after it are
calm. Ratios come from `split_ratios`, weighted by how often US companies use
them (2:1 above all; 1:10, 1:5 and 1:20 for reverse splits), and by the price
before the move: companies split forward to bring a high price down and
reverse-split to lift a low one, so the share of forward splits rises
log-linearly from 5% at $5 to 95% at $40. A split moves the median share volume
over `volume_window` bars by its ratio, within a tolerance of 0.3 on the log
scale.

Without a corporate-actions file, dorq cannot know which splits are on record,
so every split in a raw series is reported. With `--actions`, a split on file
between the two bars makes the move `explained_split` (DQ209, info); a split on
file a few sessions away, or at another ratio, makes it DQ701 or DQ703, which
report the move instead of DQ203 (doc/context.md).

With `--meta`, siblings in the move's `peer_group` that move by the same ratio on
the same day are further evidence for the split (DQ601): a move the series alone
leaves at DQ209 can become DQ203 once every series is in.

## Configuration

```toml
[price]
split_ratios = ["2:1", "3:1", "3:2", "4:1", "5:1", "5:4", "8:1", "10:1", "15:1", "20:1",
                "1:2", "1:3", "1:4", "1:5", "1:8", "1:10", "1:15", "1:20", "1:25", "1:30",
                "1:40", "1:50", "1:100"]
ratio_tolerance = 0.01

[priors]
unreported_split = 0.02
```

A ratio is new shares for old: "2:1" halves the price, "1:10" multiplies it by 10.

## Not reported

- Point series.
- A move near a split ratio followed by the volatility and volume of a real move.

## Fix

Add the split to the corporate actions (`suggested_action` gives the ratio and
the ex-date), and adjust the history if adjusted prices are wanted.
