# DQ401 volume-scale-shift

**Family:** volume · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV bars
with a volume column · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(the step is an error))

## What it reports

The volume level steps by a clean ratio, ×100, ×1000, ×1/100, and holds, with no
price change to explain it. The volume was recorded in other units (shares, round
lots of 100, thousands), or it was adjusted for a split the prices were not.
ADR 0004 notes that volume adjusted twice moves by the split ratio squared, so
4, 9, 16 and 25 are clean ratios too. Two opposite steps at the same ratio bound
an era, which is reported once.

```
LIQ04  2021-09-20  DQ401 error  volume-scale-shift  volume level steps to ×99.42 (≈ ×100) with no price change to explain it: a change of units, or volume adjusted for a split; P(error) = 0.94
      → rescale the volume of 2021-09-20..2022-12-09 by ×0.01006, or re-fetch it
LIQ60  2016-04-28..2016-12-23  DQ401 error  volume-scale-shift  volume is ×85.36 (≈ ×100) the level either side, with no price change to explain it: a change of units, or volume adjusted for a split; P(error) = 0.96
```

## The model

The level is `log(volume)` over traded bars (bars with volume above zero).

1. **Screening.** At each traded bar, compare the mean log volume of the
   `volume_window` (40) bars after it with the 40 before. A difference above
   log 1.8 marks a stretch of candidates, and the strongest step in the
   stretch is judged.
2. **The step** is the difference of the two windows' medians. Its uncertainty
   is the median's: 1.25 × MAD / √n for each window. Here n is the effective
   count, since volume runs in spells: n × (1 − ρ) / (1 + ρ), with ρ the lag-1
   autocorrelation about each window's median.
3. **Explained by the price.** If the price model found a split, scale error or
   history segment (DQ202, DQ203, DQ205) within two bars, the step is not
   judged. Nor is it when the price level moved inversely by about the same
   factor: that is a split, and DQ203 reports it.
4. **Two hypotheses.** A natural shift in activity (Student t, 4 degrees of
   freedom, scale 0.5 on the log scale, widened by the step's uncertainty)
   against an error at a clean ratio (a mixture over 100, 1000, 10, 4, 10⁶,
   9, 2, 16, 25, 3, 5, either way, each with sd 0.05 widened likewise). The
   prior on an error is 5% per step judged.

`p_error` is the posterior on the error. Steps whose `p_error` is below the info
threshold are not reported.

## Not reported

- A step that a split explains (the price moves inversely).
- A gradual change in activity: the screen needs ×1.8 across 40 bars.
- Series with fewer than 81 traded bars.

## Fix

Rescale the era's volume by the suggested factor (`suggested_action.kind` =
`rescale_volume`, with `first`, `last` and `factor`), or re-fetch it.
