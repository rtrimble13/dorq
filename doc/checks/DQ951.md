# DQ951 revision-cohort

**Family:** statements, cross-sectional (DQ95x) · **Severity:** info for revisions
alone, warn for a load fault · **Applies to:** statements, more than one series ·
**Cross-sectional**: judged once every series is in, as DQ601 is

## What it reports

Many series revised on one day, or carrying one finding in rows that became known
the same day, beyond the base rate. That is a vendor batch or a load fault, not many
separate events. The report belongs to no one series, and lists every member in
`detail.members`.

- **Revisions.** For each day, the series with a new observed version known from
  it (`knowledge_source` `observed`, or a version after the first when the source
  is unknown). The base rate is the mean count on the input's other days with
  revisions. A Poisson tail of 1e-4 or less, with `[cohort] min_series` (3)
  series at least, makes a cohort.
- **Findings.** For each code at warn or above and each day, the rows with that
  finding that became known that day (the newer of `known_from` and `loaded_on`).
  The base rate is the code's rate per row on the input's other days, with half a
  finding added. A first load that holds all of history carries most of the
  findings, and with them most of the rows, so it is no cohort of its findings. The
  same tail, with three series at least, makes a cohort.

| A day with | Reported | Members' reports |
|---|---|---|
| revisions alone: a backfill, a vendor batch | info, `market_fact` | their DQ932 and DQ903 reports on rows known that day are linked (`cohort_date`) |
| findings beyond the base rate | warn, `data_error`: a load fault | each code's reports on rows known that day are linked; DQ932 and DQ903 reports go to info, one condition reported once |

One day is one report, its revisions and its findings together.

```
(all series)  2026-10-20  DQ951 info  revision-cohort  28 series revised on 2026-10-20, against about 1.5 on a day with revisions (p = 4.6e-26): a vendor batch or a backfill, not 28 separate events: IND01, IND02, IND03, IND04, IND05, IND06 and 22 more
(all series)  2026-10-27  DQ951 warn  revision-cohort  4 series in rows known on 2026-10-27: 4 carry DQ932 (4 rows, against about 0.0016, p = 2.7e-13): a load fault, not separate findings: IND19, IND27, IND35, PRE03
```

**Keys.** The date. The detail holds `members` (every member's id), with
`series_revised`, `base_rate` and `p_value` for a day's revisions, and `codes` for
its findings.

**Output.** While DQ951 is enabled, a statements run holds its results until every
series is in, as a bars run does for DQ303, and memory grows with what would be
reported ([performance.md](../performance.md#statements-dr-0909)). The DQ951 reports
come last.

## Fix

- A backfill or vendor batch: nothing to fix. Read the members' revisions as one
  event.
- A load fault: look at that day's load in fafnir's ingestion runs, and reload the
  members' rows from the vendor once the fault is found.
