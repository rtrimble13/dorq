# DQ914 copied-period

**Family:** statements, values over time (DQ91x) · **Severity:** warn by default,
from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements · **Bayesian** (the statement value
model, [statements.md](../statements.md#the-quarterly-structure))

## What it reports

A row that repeats the period before it, to the unit, on most of its items: a
copy, where the vendor wrote last period's figures again.

- **What counts.** At least `[statements] copy_min_items` (5) of the row's items
  equal the previous period's exactly, and they are at least four in five of the
  items both rows report.
- **Flows included.** At least two of the repeats are flows. A balance can stand
  still from one quarter to the next; a quarter's revenue to the unit does not.
- **The chance.** Each item repeats as often as it does elsewhere in the series,
  between consecutive periods. The repeats to expect by chance are their sum, a
  Poisson mean. `p_error` weighs a copy (prior 0.001 a row) against the chance of
  that many repeats or more.

The row's values are not reported again as bad values (DQ911) or large changes
(DQ919).

```
IND02  2014-12-31 Q4 FY2014 income v1  DQ914 error  copied-period  the row repeats the period before it, to the unit, on 13 of 13 items: a copy, against this series' own repeats  (line 437)
```

**Keys.** The version's `record_key`. Its detail holds `items` (the repeats),
`present` (the items both rows report), `chance` and `views`.

## Fix

Re-fetch the period from its filing, and report a vendor error to the vendor.
