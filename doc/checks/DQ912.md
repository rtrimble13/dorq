# DQ912 unit-error

**Family:** statements, values over time (DQ91x) · **Severity:** warn by default,
from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements · **Bayesian** (the statement value
model, [statements.md](../statements.md#the-value-model))

## What it reports

A value, a whole row, or a run of periods stored at a power of ten: ×1,000 or
×0.001 (units for thousands, or the other way), or ×10⁶ or ×10⁻⁶.

| Scope | Found by | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| A row | a step at a power of ten between two periods taken by at least three money items and half the row, and a step back after one period | once, on the row (`scope` `row`) |
| A run of periods (an era) | the same, with the step back after two periods or more; or one item's run, two periods or more | once, on its first row, with `end_date` (`scope` `era`) |
| One value | the value model: its ratio to the value the periods around it predict is a power of ten, its size against the item's usual magnitude is, or a failing identity of the row needs it divided by one | on the value (`item`) |

A row or an era is a run of periods at one factor against the periods either side.
Every money item moving by the same factor is decisive (`p_error` 0.98). The run's
level, put right, must match the periods either side, and an item that sits at the
run's level outside it is no run. With a step out of the run but no step back, the
wrong side is the one further from the company's other statements' magnitudes in
the same periods (`p_error` 0.85).

A single value is weighed against a real change, a bad value (DQ911) and a sign
error (DQ913). The evidence: the repair fits the periods around it, an identity of
the row holds once it is repaired (`identity_repair`), the year's quarters sum to the
year once it is (`quarter_sum_repair`), and the next period goes back
(`reverts`).

```
IND05  2012-06-30 Q4 FY2012 income v1  DQ912 error  unit-error  consolidated_income is 604000000000000, expected about 633261972: stored at x1000000  (line 1327)
IND06  2015-12-26 Q1 FY2016 income v2  DQ912 error  unit-error  the row's money items are at x1000 against the periods around it (13 items)  (line 1729)
IND02  2019-12-28..2021-06-26 Q4 FY2019 balance_sheet v1  DQ912 error  unit-error  the money items of 7 quarters, 2019-12-28 to 2021-06-26, are at x0.001 against the periods either side (22 items)  (line 476)
```

**Keys.** A value's `record_key` is its version's, with `item`; a row's and an
era's add `scope` instead
(`{"statement":"income","period_type":"quarter","fiscal_date":"2015-12-26","version_no":2,"scope":"row"}`).
The detail holds `value`, `expected`, `repaired`, `power` (stored = repaired ×
10^power) and `views`, or for a row or an era `power`, `rows` and `items`.

**Versions.** Both views are judged, in force and as first reported. A ×1,000 in
version 1 that version 2 fixes is reported once, on version 1 (`views`
`first_reported`). A value both views hold alike is one report.

## Fix

Re-fetch the period from the filing named in the suggested action, and report the
vendor's value to the vendor: a unit error is the vendor's to repair.
