# DQ105 non-session-bar

**Family:** integrity · **Severity:** warn · **Applies to:** daily series ·
**Deterministic** (`p_error` = 1)

## What it reports

A bar dated on a day the calendar has no session: a weekend or a holiday on the
default `XNYS` calendar.

A handful of such bars are reported one by one. More than ten in a series are
reported as one violation, which spans them and lists the first few. That many
non-session bars is a single finding: usually a history dated a day off (DQ206),
or a calendar that does not fit the series.

```
XYZ  2024-03-09  DQ105 warn  non-session-bar  a bar on a Saturday, which is not a session on XNYS  (line 51)
```

Weekly and lower-frequency series are not checked. A weekly series dated on
Saturdays is not wrong.

## Not reported

Series that legitimately trade every day: crypto, or money-market funds that
strike a NAV seven days a week. Give them a fitting calendar (`--calendar 24x7`),
or ignore DQ105 for them with a profile. See [doc/calendar.md](../calendar.md).

## Fix

Re-date the bar if it belongs to the adjacent session, otherwise delete it.
