# DQ704 split-double-applied

**Family:** corporate actions · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:**
OHLCV bars, with `--actions` · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(the split on file is
wrong))

## What it reports

The bars move by a split's ratio twice: at its ex-date, and again within 20
sessions. The split was applied to the bars a second time, as in fafnir's BHAT,
CATB and ELOX cases. The violation spans the two moves.

```
LIQ25  2022-07-22..2022-07-28  DQ704 error  split-double-applied  split 1:20 on file ex 2022-07-22, and the close moves by its ratio twice: ×20.31 on 2022-07-22 and ×19.67 on 2022-07-28 (4 sessions later): the split applied twice; P(error) = >0.99
      → rescale 2022-07-28..2022-12-09 by ×0.05, undoing the second application
```

The second move is not reported again as DQ203. In a volatile spell, a second
move of the ratio is less surprising, and DQ704 may not reach `warn`. The price
model then reports that move as DQ203, at the right bar.

doc/checks/DQ701.md describes the model.

## Fix

Rescale the bars carrying the second application (`suggested_action.kind` =
`rescale`, with `first`, `last` and `factor`).
