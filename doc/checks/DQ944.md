# DQ944 reporting-currency

**Family:** statements, coverage and freshness (DQ94x) · **Severity:** info ·
**Applies to:** statements, with `--meta`'s `currency` · **Deterministic**

## What it reports

Statements reported in a currency (`reported_currency`) other than the one the
security trades in (`--meta`'s `currency`, matched ignoring case). An ADR or a
foreign filer reports in its home currency, so a per-share figure or a market cap
built from its statements needs the exchange rate. It succeeds fafnir's
`fundamentals_currency_mismatch`, at info: the figures are right, in their own
currency.

One report for each reporting currency, from its first period to its last.

```
IND13  2003-03-31..2026-06-30 Q1 FY2003 income  DQ944 info  reporting-currency  the statements are reported in EUR, and the security trades in USD: a per-share figure or a market cap needs the exchange rate  (line 3809)
```

**Keys.** The first period's `record_key`, without a version, with `currency`. The
detail holds `reported_currency` and `trading_currency`.

## Fix

Nothing in the data. Convert where the figures meet a price.
