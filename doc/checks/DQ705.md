# DQ705 dividend-implausible

**Family:** corporate actions · **Severity:** error, warn or info · **Applies
to:** any series, with `--actions` · **Deterministic** (`p_error` = 1; 0 for a
corroborated dividend)

## What it reports

A dividend on file that cannot be right, or is probably a slip:

- **Negative** (error).
- **Zero** (warn). It is harmless to a price adjustment, whose factor is then 1,
  but no cash dividend pays nothing: the feed lost the amount, or the event is not
  a cash dividend at all. fafnir's `core.corporate_action` allows a zero, so dorq
  reads the row and reports it rather than refusing the file.
- **At or above the close before its ex-date** (error). No company pays out more
  than a share is worth in one dividend.
- **Ten times or more off the series' others** (warn): its amount, and its yield
  on the close before it, are both at least ten times the median of the series'
  other positive dividends, or at most a tenth. A split changes the amount but not
  the yield, and a price error the yield but not the amount, so only a slip in the
  amount moves both. It needs at least three other positive dividends.
- **A change to daily accruals** (warn, once): a fund that paid monthly starts
  paying a small dividend every session. Reported once, at the first accrual
  (below).

### Corroborated by the price

A real dividend comes off the price on its ex-date; a slip in the amount does not.
For a dividend ten times off the others, dorq compares the close on the ex-date
(the first bar on or after it) with the close before less the amount. The
dividend is **corroborated** when that residual is within the larger of
`dividend_tolerance` (1.5%) of the close before and `dividend_amount_tolerance`
(25%) of the amount, and the close is not as near to the close before less the
*usual* amount. The second condition matters for small amounts: a tenth of the
usual comes off within a day's noise either way, so no close can corroborate it.

A corroborated dividend is a special dividend or a fund's distribution. It is
reported at info, as a market fact with `p_error` 0, hidden by default:

```
281  2026-10-09  DQ705 info  dividend-implausible  dividend 3.014 ex 2026-10-09 is ×27.4 the series' usual 0.11 (the median of 74 others), and ×19.85 its usual yield, but the close fell by it on the ex-date (35.57→32.72, where 35.57 less the dividend is 32.556): a special dividend or distribution
```

One that the close did not fall by stays a warning, and the message says so. A
dividend at or above the close is an error whatever the close does.

### Daily accruals

Some bond and money-market funds switch from a monthly dividend to a daily
accrual. Each accrual is a twentieth or a thirtieth of the old amount, and judged
against the monthly ones every one of them would be reported, more each night.
dorq finds the accruals: small dividends (at most a fifth of the usual amount
paid apart from others) with another such within five calendar days (a weekend's
accrual covers three days and is larger). It reports the switch once, at the first
accrual that follows regular payments, with `kind` `frequency_change`, and judges
accruals against accruals and regular payments against regular ones from then on.
If the period's total also arrives as one dividend, the period is counted twice:
the suggested action (`check_dividend_frequency`) asks to keep one or the other.

```
2085  2026-10-07  DQ705 warn  dividend-implausible  dividends from 2026-10-07 come every few days (5 so far, a median 0.001351, ×0.03244 the usual 0.04165): the fund now pays daily accruals. If the period's total also arrives as one dividend, the period is counted twice
```

On the night of the first accrual alone it is a slip at the same date, so the
record keeps its key as more accruals arrive.

### Detail

`amount` and `ex_date`; `kind`: `negative`, `zero`, `exceeds_price`, `slip`,
`special` (corroborated) or `frequency_change`; where the close is known,
`ex_date_close`, `residual` (that close less the close before less the amount)
and `corroborated`; for the ten-times branch `usual_amount`, `ratio` and
`yield_ratio`; for an accrual run `accrual_amount` and `accruals`.

```
3141  2023-05-22  DQ705 warn  dividend-implausible  dividend 0 ex 2023-05-22 pays nothing: an amount missing from the feed, or a distribution that is not cash
LIQ37  2017-02-06  DQ705 error  dividend-implausible  dividend 344 ex 2017-02-06 is at or above the close before it, 106.99 on 2017-02-03
LIQ48  2015-05-27  DQ705 warn  dividend-implausible  dividend 23 ex 2015-05-27 is ×88.46 the series' usual 0.26 (the median of 31 others), and ×100.2 its usual yield: a decimal slip, or a special dividend
```

## Configuration

```toml
[price]
dividend_tolerance = 0.015          # of the close before
dividend_amount_tolerance = 0.25    # of the amount
```

## Not reported

- A special dividend of less than ten times the usual.
- A positive dividend with no close before it in the bars, or with fewer than
  three others to compare it with. A zero or negative one needs neither.

## Fix

Check the amount (`suggested_action.kind` = `fix_dividend`). A special dividend
that really is ten times the usual can be left as it is. A zero is either an
amount to fill in from the source, or a row to delete if the event paid no cash.
