# DQ705 dividend-implausible

**Family:** corporate actions · **Severity:** error, warn or info · **Applies
to:** any series, with `--actions` · **Deterministic** (`p_error` = 1), but
**Bayesian** for a dividend ten times off the others (`p_error` = P(a slip))

## What it reports

A dividend on file that cannot be right, or is probably a slip:

- **Negative** (error).
- **Zero** (warn). It is harmless to a price adjustment, whose factor is then 1,
  but no cash dividend pays nothing: the feed lost the amount, or the event is not
  a cash dividend at all. fafnir's `core.corporate_action` allows a zero, so dorq
  reads the row and reports it rather than refusing the file.
- **At or above the close before its ex-date** (error). No company pays out more
  than a share is worth in one dividend.
- **Ten times or more off the series' others** (by probability: below): its amount, and its yield
  on the close before it, are both at least ten times the median of the series'
  other positive dividends, or at most a tenth. A split changes the amount but not
  the yield, and a price error the yield but not the amount, so only a slip in the
  amount moves both. It needs at least three other positive dividends.
- **A change to daily accruals** (warn, once): a fund that paid monthly starts
  paying a small dividend every session. Reported once, at the first accrual
  (below).

### A slip, or a special dividend

Since 0.16.0 (DE-14), a dividend ten times off the others weighs a slip (the
amount stored at the wrong scale: the error) against a real special dividend, a
distribution or a cut, with prior `[priors] dividend_slip` (a half) on the slip.
Two things tell them apart:

- **The ratio** to the usual amount. A slip's sits on a power of ten, ten to a
  thousand times either way (0.6, 0.3 and 0.1 of slips), give or take the spread
  of the series' own amounts on the log scale (at least 5%): a slip of a dividend
  twice the usual is twenty times it. A tenth of slips are no power of ten at
  all. A real one's ratio is anywhere from ten to a thousand times off, either way.
- **The ex-date close** (the first bar on or after it). A real dividend comes off
  the price; after a slip, the usual amount does. Each is a normal about its
  level, as wide as the larger of `dividend_tolerance` (1.5%) of the close before
  and `dividend_amount_tolerance` (25%) of the amount.

`p_error` is the slip's posterior, and the severity follows `[severity]`. A real
one is still reported, at info and as a market fact, hidden by default:

```
LIQ08  2016-01-04  DQ705 info  dividend-implausible  dividend 6.45 ex 2016-01-04 is ×53.75 the series' usual 0.12 (the median of 30 others), and ×17.9 its usual yield; the close went 46.13→42.56 on the ex-date, where less the dividend is 39.68 and less the usual 46.01: a special dividend or distribution (or a cut) more than a slip; P(error) = 0.2
LIQ03  2018-10-02  DQ705 error  dividend-implausible  dividend 18 ex 2018-10-02 is ×138.5 the series' usual 0.13 (the median of 30 others), and ×101.2 its usual yield; the close went 61.75→60.63 on the ex-date, where less the dividend is 43.75 and less the usual 61.62: an amount stored wrong (a slip) more than a special dividend; P(error) = >0.99
```

Before 0.16.0 a dividend was a special one only when the close fell within the
tolerance of it, and a warning otherwise: a special dividend on a day that also
moved 3% was reported as a slip (on dorq-synth's universe, 0-3 a universe, against
0-1 now). The ratio now counts too: 27 times the usual, no power of ten, is a
special one more than a slip unless the close says otherwise. Some cases stay
ambiguous: a special on a day that moves about as much again, a slip on a day that
falls by about the slipped amount, or a dividend history so spread (0.03 to 0.16 a
quarter) that ten times one of its amounts is fifteen times its median.

A dividend at or above the close is an error whatever the close does.

### Daily accruals

Some bond and money-market funds switch from a monthly dividend to a daily
accrual. Each accrual is a twentieth or a thirtieth of the old amount, and judged
against the monthly ones every one of them would be reported, more each night.
dorq finds the accruals: small dividends (at most a fifth of the usual amount
paid apart from others) with another such within five calendar days (a weekend's
accrual covers three days and is larger). It reports the switch once, at the first
accrual that follows regular payments, with `kind` `frequency_change`, and judges
accruals against accruals and regular payments against regular ones from then on.
If the period's total also arrives as one dividend, the period is counted twice:
the suggested action (`check_dividend_frequency`) asks to keep one or the other.

```
2085  2026-10-07  DQ705 warn  dividend-implausible  dividends from 2026-10-07 come every few days (5 so far, a median 0.001351, ×0.03244 the usual 0.04165): the fund now pays daily accruals. If the period's total also arrives as one dividend, the period is counted twice
```

On the night of the first accrual alone it is a dividend a twentieth to a
thirtieth of the usual at the same date, which is no power of ten: a cut more
than a slip, at info (since 0.16.0). The record keeps its key as more accruals
arrive.

### Detail

`amount` and `ex_date`; `kind`: `negative`, `zero`, `exceeds_price`, `slip` (a
slip more than a special one), `special` (a special dividend, distribution or cut
more than a slip) or `frequency_change`; where the close is known,
`ex_date_close`, `residual` (that close less the close before less the amount)
and `corroborated` (the close fell within the tolerance of the dividend and not
of the usual amount: the rule before 0.16.0, kept as a field); for the ten-times
branch `usual_amount`, `ratio` and
`yield_ratio`; for an accrual run `accrual_amount` and `accruals`.

```
3141  2023-05-22  DQ705 warn  dividend-implausible  dividend 0 ex 2023-05-22 pays nothing: an amount missing from the feed, or a distribution that is not cash
LIQ37  2017-02-06  DQ705 error  dividend-implausible  dividend 344 ex 2017-02-06 is at or above the close before it, 106.99 on 2017-02-03
```

## Configuration

```toml
[price]
dividend_tolerance = 0.015          # of the close before
dividend_amount_tolerance = 0.25    # of the amount

[priors]
dividend_slip = 0.5                 # a dividend ten times off the others is a slip
```

## Not reported

- A special dividend of less than ten times the usual.
- A positive dividend with no close before it in the bars, or with fewer than
  three others to compare it with. A zero or negative one needs neither.

## Fix

Check the amount (`suggested_action.kind` = `fix_dividend`). A special dividend
that really is ten times the usual can be left as it is. A zero is either an
amount to fill in from the source, or a row to delete if the event paid no cash.
