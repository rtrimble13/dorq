# DQ941 period-gap

**Family:** statements, coverage and freshness (DQ94x) · **Severity:** warn by
default; a change of fiscal year and a period not yet filed are info · **Applies
to:** statements; `--meta`'s trading life bounds it
([statements.md](../statements.md#coverage-and-freshness))

## What it reports

A break in a statements series' quarterly or annual sequence. Each period type's
sequence is read from its period ends, any statement or version, and their labels
(`fiscal_period`). Quarters are one apart at 56 to 126 days, and years at 300 to 430
(`[statements] quarter_spacing`, `year_spacing`).

| Hypothesis | Evidence | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| `missing_period` | a gap of two periods or more, the labels skipping with it | warn, one report on the first missing period, with `end_date` for more |
| `fiscal_year_change` | a year that is not one apart from the year before (a transition of a few months); quarters whose labels rebase near it | info, `market_fact` |
| `mislabel` | one quarter apart, but the label skips (Q1 then Q3), and no period is missing | warn, on the mislabelled quarter |
| `not_yet_filed` | a missing period that would be filed after the series' latest knowledge date | info |

- **While the security traded.** A missing period that ends before the coverage
  floor (`[statements] coverage_floor`, 1998), or outside the security's trading
  life in `--meta` (`first_trade_date`, `last_trade_date`, `delisted_date`), is not
  reported.
- **A mislabel's next quarter** is judged against the label the mislabelled quarter
  should have had. So one wrong label is one report, where the labels after it
  resume the cycle.
- **A missing quarter's end** is estimated between the periods either side, on a
  month end where they are on one.

```
IND01  2007-06-30 Q3 FY2007 income  DQ941 warn  period-gap  1 quarter missing between 2007-03-31 and 2007-09-30 (183 days), while the security traded  (line 100)
IND03  2008-09-30 Q4 FY2008 income  DQ941 warn  period-gap  the quarter ending 2008-09-30 is labelled Q4 one quarter after Q2: Q3 was due, and no period is missing (the labels after it resume the cycle)  (line 657)
IND19  2018-09-30 FY2018 income  DQ941 info  period-gap  a change of fiscal year: the year ending 2018-09-30 is a transition of 9 months  (line 5867)
```

**Keys.** The period's `record_key`, without a version, with its fiscal date the
missing (or mislabelled) period's. The detail holds `hypothesis` and
`periods_missing`.

## Fix

- A missing period: load it from its filing (fafnir's `fundamentals_gap` was the
  same finding), or, where the issuer never filed it, record why.
- A mislabel: correct the version's `fiscal_period` from the filing.
- A change of fiscal year: nothing. A trailing-twelve-month figure across it needs
  the transition period's own length.
