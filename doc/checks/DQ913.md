# DQ913 sign-error

**Family:** statements, values over time (DQ91x) · **Severity:** warn by default,
from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements · **Bayesian** (the statement value
model, [statements.md](../statements.md#the-value-model)) · **Needs:** `--items`

## What it reports

A value whose sign is flipped: its negative is what the periods around it
predict, to within a factor of two, on an item that keeps one sign.

A sign is judged only on an item `--items` gives a convention (`positive_is_inflow`
or `positive_is_outflow`, not `signed`), that is not a subtotal (a subtotal's sign
follows its terms'), and that has the other sign in at least 19 of every 20 of its
other periods (`sign_history`). Revenue, a cost, capital expenditure, dividends
paid and depreciation are such items; net income and the cash flow's subtotals
are not.

The evidence is the value model's (DQ911): an identity or the year's quarter sum
that holds with the sign put right, the next period going back, and the row's
other items left as they were.

```
IND09  2003-06-30 Q1 FY2004 cash_flow v1  DQ913 error  sign-error  capital_expenditure is 12627000, expected about -12356245: its sign is flipped  (line 2530)
IND16  2014-09-30 Q3 FY2014 income v1  DQ913 error  sign-error  revenue is -58572000, expected about 57731614: its sign is flipped  (line 4853)
```

**Keys.** The version's `record_key`, with `item`. The detail holds `value`,
`expected`, `repaired` (the value negated) and `views`.

**fafnir.** DQ902 reports a negative where none can be (revenue, total assets, a
share count) whatever its size; DQ913 reports a sign flipped against the item's
own history, on any item with a convention, and names the repair.

## Fix

Check the value's sign against the filing, and report a vendor error to the
vendor.
