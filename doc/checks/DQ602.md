# DQ602 market-day

**Family:** cross-sectional · **Severity:** info · **Applies to:** the market
reference, with `--market` · market fact

## What it reports

A day the market reference moved five or more standard deviations of its own
recent moves. The volatility is a discounted estimate, about 33 sessions,
started from the robust scale of all its moves. On such a day every series'
ordinary move is wider (doc/context.md): what remains of a market move once beta
is taken out carries an error of about half the market's move. Correlated moves
on a crash day are therefore not reported as errors.

```
(all series)  2020-02-10  DQ602 info  market-day  the market (SPY) moved −13.5% (26 standard deviations of its recent moves): every series' ordinary move is wider today
```

This is not a violation. It is reported at info, so that a reader can see why a
day's large moves were not reported.

Without `--market`, a broad enough cross-section could stand in for the market.
That needs every series read before any is judged, which streaming input rules
out, so dorq does not do it.
