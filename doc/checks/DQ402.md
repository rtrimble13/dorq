# DQ402 volume-spike-no-move

**Family:** volume · **Severity:** info · **Applies to:** OHLCV bars with a
volume column · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(a recording error))

## What it reports

Volume ten times its usual level or more, on a day the price did not move. Real
spikes happen: index rebalances, block trades, options expiry. So this is info,
classified as a market fact unless the spike lands on a clean ratio (×100,
×1000) that a recording error would make.

```
LIQ31  2022-03-10  DQ402 info  volume-spike-no-move  volume 65,465,284 is ×14.4 the usual 4,558,906, on a day the close moved 0.8 standard deviations; P(error) = 0.01
```

## The model

- **Usual volume:** the median of the `volume_window` (40) traded bars before.
- **A spike:** at least ×10 the usual, and at least 4 robust standard deviations
  (the MAD of log volume, at least 0.3) above it.
- **No move:** the bar's return is within 1.5 standard deviations of the price
  model's volatility (doc/checks/DQ201.md).
- **`p_error`:** an error lands on a clean ratio (the DQ401 mixture, sd 0.1),
  with prior 10%. A real spike's size above ×10 is exponential with scale 1 on
  the log scale.

Bars inside a DQ401 era, or within the window after a DQ401 step, are not
reported: the step explains them.

## Fix

Usually none. Ignore DQ402 if spikes are expected (`ignore = ["DQ402"]`).
