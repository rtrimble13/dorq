# DQ942 statements-stale

**Family:** statements, coverage and freshness (DQ94x) · **Severity:** warn by
default, from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements, with `--as-of`; `--meta`'s
trading life · **Bayesian** ([statements.md](../statements.md#coverage-and-freshness))

## What it reports

A statements series with no newer period by the as-of date, judged by the issuer's
own cadence and filing lag rather than a fixed span (fafnir's `fundamentals_stale`
used 200 days).

- **The next period.** The latest quarter known by the as-of date (the latest year,
  for a series of years only), plus a quarter (or a year). It is a 10-K's when a year
  ends about then, the latest year known by the as-of date a year before it. In a
  series with no years, it is a 10-K's when the latest quarter is Q3.
- **When it is due.** The next period's end, plus the issuer's usual lag for that
  form: the median of its own filed first versions (with a spread of 4 days at
  least), or the SEC's deadlines (42 days for a 10-Q, 75 for a 10-K) where it has
  fewer than four.
- **How late.** The chance that a filing on time is still to come is the issuer's
  spread, with a fat tail for the late filings every issuer has now and then. A
  delinquent or a stopped feed has a prior of 0.02. `p_error` is its probability
  given that nothing has come by the as-of date.
- **No longer trading.** A security whose last trade (or delisting) is more than 30
  days before the as-of date, or that `--meta` marks inactive, files no more, and is
  not stale.

```
IND10  2025-09-30..2026-10-30 Q3 FY2025 income  DQ942 error  statements-stale  no period after 2025-09-30 by 2026-10-30: the next, ending 2025-12-30 (a 10-K), was due about 2026-02-21 at this issuer's usual 53 days, 251 days ago  (line 3211)
```

**Keys.** The latest period's `record_key`, without a version: the report stays the
same while the series stays stale, and becomes another once a newer period
arrives, as DQ304's does. The detail holds `latest_fiscal_date`,
`next_period_end`, `due`, `as_of` and `days_overdue`.

**Without `--as-of`** there is no date to be stale by, and DQ942 judges nothing.

## Fix

Check whether the issuer filed the period and the vendor has it. An issuer that is
delinquent (no 10-Q or 10-K, or an NT filing) is a fact to record. A filing the
vendor lacks is a feed to chase.
