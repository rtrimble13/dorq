# DQ916 cumulative-quarters

**Family:** statements, values over time (DQ91x) · **Severity:** warn by default,
from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements · **Needs:** `--items` (the flows)

## What it reports

A fiscal year's quarters stored year to date, as 10-Qs report them, or one quarter
holding its year's figures. It judges each fiscal year with exactly four quarters
and one year in force, on the income and cash flow statements, for each flow
`--items` lists whose quarters fail to sum to the year:

| Hypothesis | A flow fits it when |
|---|---|
| `cumulative_quarter` | Q4 equals the year, and Q1 and each quarter less the one before are quarters: of the year's sign, and a twentieth to 70% of it |
| `annual_in_quarter` | one quarter equals the year, and the year less the other three is a quarter |

A year is reported when at least three flows, and half of those judged, fit one:
`p_error` 0.97 (or 0.95 for a quarter holding the year) where two thirds fit, 0.9
otherwise. Quarters to date are one report, on Q2, with `end_date` Q4.

A year with two years in force (a change of fiscal year that labels a transition
period with the year it shares: the shape behind rtrimble13/fafnir#217), or with
another count of quarters, is not judged. The rows reported are not reported again
as bad values (DQ911) or large changes (DQ919). DQ904 reports the same years'
quarter sums failing.

```
IND04  2006-03-31..2006-09-30 Q2 FY2006 cash_flow v1  DQ916 error  cumulative-quarters  the quarters of FY2006 are stored year to date: Q2 to Q4 hold the sums from Q1, and Q4 the year (7 of 10 flows)  (line 897)
IND02  2010-09-30 Q3 FY2010 income v1  DQ916 error  cumulative-quarters  the quarter holds FY2010's flows (13 of 13 equal the year's)  (line 374)
```

**Keys.** The version's `record_key` (Q2's for quarters to date). Its detail holds
`fiscal_year`, `items` (the flows that fit) and `present` (those judged).

**fafnir.** DQ916 succeeds `fundamentals_ytd_suspect`, which flags a year whose
four quarters of revenue or operating cash flow sum to more than 1.5 years. DQ916
judges every flow, tells quarters to date from a quarter holding the year, and
names the rows.

## Fix

Re-fetch the quarters from their filings: a 10-Q's year-to-date column is not the
quarter's. Report a vendor error to the vendor.
