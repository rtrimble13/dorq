# DQ701 split-misdated

**Family:** corporate actions · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:**
OHLCV bars, with `--actions` · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(the split on file is
wrong))

## What it reports

A split on file whose ratio the bars move by a few sessions from its ex-date, and
not at it. The split is real; its date is wrong. A misdated split mis-adjusts
the history between the two dates.

```
LIQ02  2022-01-24  DQ701 error  split-misdated  split 2:1 on file ex 2022-01-24, but the close moves ×0.497 on 2022-01-19, 3 sessions earlier, and not on the ex-date (×0.991); P(error) = >0.99
      → move the 2:1 split from ex 2022-01-24 to ex 2022-01-19
      hypotheses: misdated >0.999 no_jump <0.001 double_applied <0.001 confirmed <0.001 ratio_mismatch <0.001
```

The move it points to is not reported again as DQ203.

## The model

DQ701–DQ704 share one model, which judges every split on file against the bars
around its ex-date. The ex-date's bar is the first bar on or after the ex-date,
so a split whose ex-date falls between two stored bars is judged at the next one.
Five explanations are compared:

| Verdict | The bars | Prior |
|---|---|---|
| `confirmed` | move by the split's ratio at the ex-date's bar | 1 − `split_on_file_error` |
| `misdated` (DQ701) | move by it at one bar within 5 sessions either side instead, each equally likely | `split_on_file_error` / 4 |
| `no_jump` (DQ702) | do not move by it | `split_on_file_error` / 4 |
| `ratio_mismatch` (DQ703) | move at the ex-date by another clean ratio, or by this one inverted | `split_on_file_error` / 4 |
| `double_applied` (DQ704) | move by it at the ex-date and again at one bar within 20 sessions | `split_on_file_error` / 4 |

`split_on_file_error` is 0.06 (`[priors]`). Under every verdict the other moves in
the 20 sessions either side are ordinary, so only the bars that differ enter the
comparison. A move "by the ratio" is the ratio exactly, plus an ordinary move with
`ratio_tolerance` of slack. An ordinary move is the price model's: Student t with
a small chance of a jump, against the volatility either side of the bar,
leaving out the bar itself (doc/checks/DQ201.md). The wrong ratio on file is the
ratio inverted (2:1 entered as 1:2) with weight 0.3, or another of
`split_ratios`, weighted by how often companies use it.

`p_error` is 1 − P(`confirmed`). Each check reports the splits whose most
probable error is its own. When fewer than `provisional_bars` bars follow the
moves, the report is provisional and at most a warning.

## Fix

Move the split to the date the bars show (`suggested_action.kind` =
`redate_split`, with `ex_date` and `new_ex_date`).
