# DQ305 frequency-gap

**Family:** coverage · **Severity:** warn · **Applies to:** weekly, monthly,
quarterly and annual series · **Deterministic** (`p_error` = 1)

## What it reports

Missing periods in a low-frequency series, such as a monthly series with no
observation for April. Each gap is one violation. Its date is the start of the
first missing period, and `end_date` is the start of the last.

```
CPI  2023-04-01..2023-05-01  DQ305 warn  frequency-gap  2 monthly observations missing between 2023-03-01 and 2023-06-01
```

The frequency is inferred from the dates (see `frequency` below). A series
counts as weekly, monthly, quarterly or annual when the lower quartile of its
gaps is 5–10, 25–35, 80–100 or 350–380 days. Periods are calendar weeks
(Monday-based), months, quarters and years. So a monthly series observed on the
1st one month and the 28th the next is not a gap.

## Configuration

```toml
[coverage]
frequency = "auto"   # or force "monthly" etc.; a profile can set it per series
```

## Fix

Re-fetch the missing periods. A source that skips a period on purpose (a
discontinued release) is a fact to accept.
