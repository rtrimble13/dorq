# DQ303 cohort-gap

**Family:** coverage (cross-sectional) · **Severity:** error · **Applies to:**
daily series, across the whole input · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = 1 − tail
probability)

## What it reports

A session that many series miss at once, where each of them normally has a bar on
nearly every session. One series with a gap may be a market fact. Two hundred with
a gap on the same date is a missed load, and it should be reported once, not two
hundred times. fafnir's sweep policy calls `cohort_size` "the whole test" for
exactly this reason; DQ303 automates it.

```
(all series)  2023-06-15  DQ303 error  cohort-gap  20 of 30 series that have a bar on nearly every session have none on this one, where healthy feeds would explain about 0.1; a failed load, not 20 separate gaps
```

A series counts as *expected* on a session when its healthy density there
(DQ301's model) is above `confident_density` (0.95). On each session dorq counts
two things:

- *n*: the series expected that day;
- *k*: how many of those have no bar.

Without a failed load, *k* is roughly Poisson with mean λ. λ is the sum of each
expected series' healthy miss probability, plus *n* times the stationary outage
share. A session is a cohort when *k* ≥ `cohort_min_series` (3) and P(*X* ≥ *k*)
≤ `cohort_max_tail` (1e-6).

When nearly every expected series is missing (90% or more), the message also
suggests the calendar: an unlisted market closure looks exactly like that.

Each member's DQ301 run (or DQ304 stale tail) that the cohort explains is
downgraded to info, and its message names the cohort. Series that stop before
the as-of date count on the session after their last bar, so a load that failed
for many series on the latest night is a cohort too.

The violation belongs to no single series. In JSON its `series` is `null`; in
the fafnir format its `security_id` is `null`. It comes after every per-series
violation.

## Configuration

```toml
[coverage]
cohort_min_series = 3
cohort_max_tail = 1e-06
confident_density = 0.95
```

These settings are global; a profile cannot set them.

## Fix

Re-run the load for that date. For a closure the calendar does not know, pass a
calendar that does (`--calendar-file`).
