# DQ403 move-on-zero-volume

**Family:** volume · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV bars
with a volume column · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(the bar is wrong))

## What it reports

A bar whose close moved from the close before, at least half a step of the price
grid, on zero volume, on a series where that is rare. With no trade, the close
should not move. A moved close is an indicative quote, a lost volume, or a
stale carry that a later correction moved.

```
LIQ06  2016-02-24  DQ403 error  move-on-zero-volume  close 28.43→28.49 on zero volume, where 0 other bars of 1,992 move with no volume: an indicative quote, or a lost volume; P(error) = 0.98
      → re-fetch the bar of 2016-02-24
```

## The model

Some feeds mark every untraded session with a quote. On those, zero-volume
moves are a habit, not an error. The check counts the series' other zero-volume
moves and its bars with a volume. Their rate, `(others + 0.5) / (bars + 1)`, is
the chance that a healthy bar does this. The prior on an error is 1%:
`p_error = 0.01 / (0.01 + 0.99 × rate)`. One such bar in two thousand is reported
at error. One in ten is not reported.

The grid is the exchange's tick for the date and price (or the `tick_size` in
`--meta`), or the grid most closes are written on, whichever is coarser.

## Not reported

- A zero-volume bar that repeats the previous close: a carry bar (DQ502).
- With `--actions`, a zero-volume bar that moves by the ratio of a split on file
  between it and the bar before: the feed carried the last close, adjusted.
- Series where moves on zero volume are common.

## Fix

Re-fetch the bar (`suggested_action.kind` = `refetch_bar`).
