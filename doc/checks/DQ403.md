# DQ403 move-on-zero-volume

**Family:** volume · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV bars
with a volume column · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(the bar is wrong))

## What it reports

A bar whose close moved from the close before, at least half a step of the price
grid, on zero volume, where a lost volume is likelier than the alternatives. With
no trade, the close should not move. A moved close is a lost volume (a traded bar
whose volume the feed dropped), an indicative quote, or the feed's habit.

```
LIQ13  2016-10-24  DQ403 error  move-on-zero-volume  close 34.08→35.07 on zero volume, 99 steps of the 0.01 grid where an ordinary move is 1.6%: a lost volume, more than an indicative quote or the feed's habit, on a series that trades about 1200 times a day, with 0 other such bars of 1,999; P(error) = >0.99
      → re-fetch the bar of 2016-10-24
```

## The model

Since 0.16.0 (DE-14), each such bar weighs three explanations of its move `r`
(the log of the close over the close before):

| Explanation | Prior, per bar | The move |
|---|---|---|
| **A lost volume** (the error) | `[priors] lost_volume` (0.001) | an ordinary one: Student t with 4 degrees of freedom, of the sd of the price model's ordinary move to the bar (the series' typical daily move when it scored none), with the bid-ask bounce of 0.7 steps at least |
| **An indicative quote**: a mark posted on a day with no trade | the series' quote rate, below | within about a step of the last close: normal, of sd `[price] quote_steps` (1) steps of the grid |
| **The feed's habit**: a feed that reports no volume on some days that traded | the series' habit rate, below | an ordinary one |

`p_error` is the lost volume's share of the three.

The **quote rate** is pooled with the series' class. The class rate is the floor
`[priors] indicative_quote` (0.0001), for a series that always trades, plus the
chance of a day with no trade, e^−(trades a day), where trades a day is the
median volume on traded days over `[coverage] trade_size`; at most a half. A
series' own bars with no volume within a quote's reach (two `quote_steps` or
fewer, carry bars that repeat the close among them: marks that held) are shrunk
to it with `[price] quote_strength` (50) bars: `(own + 50 × class) / (bars +
50)`. A thin name that is untraded one day in six has quotes; a name that trades
two thousand times a day does not, however few its bars.

The **habit rate** is the series' other zero-volume moves beyond a quote's
reach, shrunk to the same floor the same way. A feed that reports no volume one
day in eight has a habit, and its zero-volume bars are not each an error.

So a one-cent move on zero volume is a quote on a thin name and a lost volume on
a liquid one, and a move of an ordinary size on zero volume is a lost volume
unless the series does it often. Before 0.16.0, one probability, `0.01 / (0.01 +
0.99 × rate)` from the series' own rate alone, served every bar: a thin name's
first quote was reported at warn. On dorq-synth's universe, whose thin names
carry 4 quotes each, 69-77 of them were reported at warn on seeds 1-8; none is now.

The grid is the exchange's tick for the date and price (or the `tick_size` in
`--meta`), or the grid most closes are written on, whichever is coarser.

`detail` carries `grid_steps` (the move in steps), `move_sd`, `trades_a_day`,
`quote_rate`, `habit_rate`, `other_zero_volume_moves` and `hypothesis`
(`lost_volume`, `indicative_quote` or `feed_habit`, the likeliest of the three).

## Not reported

- A zero-volume bar that repeats the previous close: a carry bar (DQ502).
- With `--actions`, a zero-volume bar that moves by the ratio of a split on file
  between it and the bar before: the feed carried the last close, adjusted.
- Indicative quotes, and a feed's habit, where those are likelier than a lost
  volume (above).

## Configuration

```toml
[priors]
lost_volume = 0.001        # a traded bar's volume lost, per bar
indicative_quote = 0.0001  # a moved zero-volume mark, per bar, on a series that always trades

[price]
quote_steps = 1            # a mark's move from the last close, in grid steps (sd)
quote_strength = 50        # the class rate's weight, in bars, against the series' own
```

## Fix

Re-fetch the bar (`suggested_action.kind` = `refetch_bar`).
