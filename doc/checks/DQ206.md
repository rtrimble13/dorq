# DQ206 date-shift

**Family:** price action · **Severity:** by probability (error at the default
thresholds) · **Applies to:** daily series with at least 60 bars ·
**Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(shift))

## What it reports

A history dated one day off: every bar carries the date of the day before (or
after) the session it belongs to. fafnir found two such histories, FVI and WLL.
They had no Friday bars, and Sunday bars that were really Monday sessions.

The check compares the bars' weekday counts with the calendar's sessions over the
same span, under three hypotheses: the dates are right, they are a day late, or
they are a day early. Under each one, the calendar's sessions predict how often
each weekday should appear. Each hypothesis allows 1% of bars to fall anywhere,
so a stray bar does not decide anything. The prior is 1% for each shift
direction. The posterior probability of a shift is `p_error`.

```
FVI  2009-01-02..2015-06-30  DQ206 error  date-shift  weekday counts fit dates one day earlier than the sessions they belong to (bars Mon 0 Tue 312 Wed 318 Thu 315 Fri 0 Sat 0 Sun 309; XNYS sessions Mon 291 ...); shift the history forward a day; P(shift) = >0.99
```

`detail.shift_days` is the correction: +1 moves every date a day later.

A calendar with sessions on every day (`24x7`) cannot show a shift, so the check
does not run under one. Only whole histories are judged. When only part of a
history slipped, DQ105 reports its non-session bars.

## Fix

Re-date the history with fafnir's `prices shift --days N`, after reading the dry
run's weekday table.
