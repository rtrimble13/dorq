# DQ101 ohlc-bounds

**Family:** integrity · **Severity:** error · **Applies to:** OHLCV bars ·
**Deterministic** (`p_error` = 1)

## What it reports

A bar whose high is below its open, low or close, or whose low is above its open
or close. No trade can print outside the session's own range, so the bar is wrong.
The cause is usually one field taken from a different bar, or one field on a
different scale.

One violation per bar. The message lists every relation that fails, and `detail`
holds all four prices.

```
AAPL  2024-03-05  DQ101 error  ohlc-bounds  high 9.5 < close 10  (line 42)
```

## Not reported

A bar with a missing or unparseable price. DQ104 reports those.

## Fix

Re-fetch the bar from the source. If the source still serves it, delete the bar and
let the gap be reported.
