# DQ204 ohlc-close-mismatch

**Family:** price action · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV
bars · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(an error))

## What it reports

The close is wrong and the rest of the bar is right: the close lies outside a
range that holds the open, and open, high and low sit at the level the series was
at, which the next bar returns to.

```
LIQ06  2020-08-24  DQ204 error  ohlc-close-mismatch  close 2.1 outside the bar's range 20.95..21.47, whose open 20.99 held the prior close 20.86; P(error) = >0.99
```

## The model

The `bad_close` hypothesis of the price model (doc/checks/DQ201.md): a bad print
of one bar in the close field alone. Its evidence: the close outside
[low, high] when the range holds the open (a genuine bar's close is inside its
range; P = 0.9 under `bad_close` against 0.001), the median of open, high and low
near the previous close rather than the new one, and the next bar back at the
level.

DQ101 also reports a close outside the bar's range. DQ204 adds which field is
wrong. When the open is outside the range too, the high or low is the bad field,
and only DQ101 reports it.

## Configuration

```toml
[priors]
bad_close = 0.01
```

## Not reported

- Bars without open, high and low.
- A bar whose open, high and low moved with the close (DQ201).

## Fix

Re-fetch the bar's close.
