# DQ919 large-change

**Family:** statements, values over time (DQ91x) · **Severity:** info ·
**Applies to:** statements · **Bayesian** (the statement value model,
[statements.md](../statements.md#the-value-model))

## What it reports

A change the value model calls real (an acquisition, a write-down, a business
sold), kept for review: the successor of the threshold that would have flagged
it.

It is a value the model screened (far from its prediction, five times or a fifth
of both neighbours, or a sign change) where `real_change` is likelier than every
error. Only a change large for its item is reported: at least double or half the
expected value, at least eight times the item's local scale out, on an item whose
values rarely stray (fewer than one in twenty of its periods). A real change of
the size the item often makes is not reported at all.

```
IND25  2018-09-30 Q3 FY2018 balance_sheet v1  DQ919 info  large-change  goodwill_and_intangibles is 76191000, expected about 182596793 (0.4 times): a change the evidence calls real  (line 7457)
IND33  2013-12-31 Q4 FY2013 balance_sheet v1  DQ919 info  large-change  goodwill_and_intangibles is 394983000, expected about 192314935 (2.1 times): a change the evidence calls real  (line 10066)
```

Above, from dorq-synth: goodwill written down, and goodwill doubling.

The classification is `market_fact`. `p_error` is the model's probability of an
error, which is below that of a real change. The hypotheses and evidence are as
for DQ911.

**Keys.** The version's `record_key`, with `item`. The detail holds `value`,
`expected`, `score` and `views`.

## Fix

Nothing to fix. Read it as context: a step in the series that later checks and
models take as given.
