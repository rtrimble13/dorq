# DQ202 scale-shift

**Family:** price action · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV
bars and positive point series · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(an error))

## What it reports

The level changes by a power of ten, or to a price no security trades at,
without the volume shift a split would cause. Either an era stored at the wrong
scale (cents as dollars, a split applied backwards) or a scale change that runs
to the end of the series. Two opposite shifts are reported once, as the era
between them.

```
LIQ13  2020-04-30..2021-02-08  DQ202 error  scale-shift  closes 2020-04-30..2021-02-08 are ×0.0009734 the level either side (×0.001): an era at the wrong scale; P(error) = >0.99
LIQ01  2022-12-09  DQ202 warn  scale-shift  close 25.54→2503 (×98 ≈ ×100); P(error) = >0.99, provisional
```

## The model

The `scale_error` hypothesis of the price model (doc/checks/DQ201.md): the move is
a power of ten (±1 to ±6, weighted toward 10 and 100) plus an ordinary return,
and volume stays where it was. Evidence for it over a split is the volume level:
a split moves share volume by its ratio, a scale error does not. A price outside
[`min_price`, `max_price`] (1e-5 to 1e6) on one side, or a change of three or
more in the decimals the closes are written with (DQ106's signal), is evidence
too.

The suggested action rescales the era. For an unpaired shift, the era is the side
whose price is implausible, or otherwise the shorter side.

## Configuration

```toml
[price]
ratio_tolerance = 0.01   # slack on a clean ratio, on the log scale
min_price = 1e-05
max_price = 1000000

[priors]
scale_error = 0.02
```

## Not reported

- A level shift with the inverse volume shift of a split (DQ203).
- A shift that reverts within five bars (DQ201).

## Fix

Rescale the era by the factor in `suggested_action`, or re-fetch it.
