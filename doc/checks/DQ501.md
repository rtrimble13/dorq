# DQ501 repeated-price

**Family:** stale values · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV
bars with a volume column · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(a stale feed))

## What it reports

The same close on traded bars running (volume above zero), more often than the
series' own moves allow. A feed that stopped updating repeats the last price
while volume keeps arriving. The plan's example is EQC's 0.9475 three days
running on 3.7–5.8M shares.

```
LIQ13  2019-09-25..2019-10-01  DQ501 error  repeated-price  close 36.1 on 6 traded bars running (from 2019-09-24), on volume 1,644,004–2,451,193, where the series' moves make that 1 in 58,149,434,512; P(error) = >0.99 (5 whole bars repeated)
      → re-fetch 2019-09-25..2019-10-01; a stale feed repeated the close
```

The violation spans the repeats; the bar they repeat is named after "from".

## The model

For each repeat in the run, the chance that a healthy series leaves its close
unchanged is `q`, the larger of two estimates:

- **The return model:** the chance that the price moves less than half a step of
  the grid, `P(|Δ| < step / 2)`. It uses the price model's volatility from either
  side of the run (doc/checks/DQ201.md), and leaves out the bid-ask bounce. The
  grid is the exchange's tick, or the lattice the prices nearby sit on. That
  lattice is read 20 bars either side of the run, and the coarser kept.
- **The series' own habit:** how often closes repeat in the 40 bars either side,
  as a chain. The first repeat uses the share of moves followed by a repeat;
  later ones use the share of repeats followed by another. The model's `q`
  counts as 10 bars of prior.

A healthy run has probability `∏ q`; a stale feed starts at a bar with prior
`stale_run` (3 × 10⁻⁵). `p_error` is the posterior on the stale feed.

Prices 20 grid steps wide or fewer are not judged: a sub-dime stock quoted in
cents, or a stock in sixteenths, seldom moves a whole step, and runs are how it
trades. "Whole bars repeated" counts repeats whose open, high and low repeat as
well. It is reported, but not used as evidence: on a quiet low-priced stock they
repeat with the close.

**A pinned price** is not judged either, by DQ107's rule: a step of the grid is
under `pinned_step_share` (0.002) of the price, and the traded closes in the 40
bars either side of the run move a median of `flat_bar_steps` (3) steps or fewer.
A SPAC at its trust value ($10.06 eight days running) repeats its close because
its price does not move. A liquid name whose closes move many steps a day, such
as EQC's 0.9475 three days running on 3.7–5.8M shares, is still judged.

**A thin series**, by DQ107's floors (`flat_bar_min_median_volume`, 5,000 shares,
and `flat_bar_min_median_dollar_volume`), has its runs reported at info: a few
prints a day at one price repeat the close.

`detail` gives `close`, `bars`, `whole_bars_repeated`,
`log_probability_healthy`, `grid_step`, `thin_series`, and `close_steps` (the
median move of the closes nearby, in steps) when there are enough of them.

## Configuration

```toml
[priors]
stale_run = 3e-05

[integrity]                   # shared with DQ107
pinned_step_share = 0.002
flat_bar_steps = 3
flat_bar_min_median_volume = 5000
flat_bar_min_median_dollar_volume = 0
```

## Not reported

- Coarse prices, and pinned prices, as above. Thin series' runs are info.
- Repeats on zero volume: carry bars (DQ502).
- A short run on a quiet stock: two or three repeats happen now and then, so
  they are reported at info, if at all. The longer the run, and the more the
  stock moves, the surer the report.

## Fix

Re-fetch the run (`suggested_action.kind` = `delete_bars`).
