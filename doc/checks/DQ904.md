# DQ904 period-tie

**Family:** statements, integrity and identities (DQ90x) · **Severity:** warn by
default, from `p_error` · **Applies to:** statements · **Needs:**
`[statements] cash_tie`, or `--items` for the quarter sums

## What it reports

A tie across periods that fails: what no identity within one row can see
([statements.md](../statements.md#identities-and-ties)). It reads the periods in
force (each period's open version).

| Tie | Judged | Reported on |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash.** The change in `cash_tie`'s first item between two consecutive balance sheets against the later period's change on the cash flow (the second item) | quarters `quarter_spacing` apart, and years `year_spacing` apart, with a cash flow for the later | the cash flow, or one balance sheet (below) |
| **Quarter sums.** A fiscal year's four quarters against its year, for each flow item `--items` lists on the income and cash flow statements | a fiscal year with exactly four quarters and one year in force, the quarters within the year | the quarter out of line, or the year |

**Cash.** The tie holds within the larger of `cash_tie_share` (2%) of the larger
balance and `tie_floor`. From `restricted_cash_from` (2018-01-01; ASU 2016-18) the
cash flow reconciles cash and restricted cash, so it holds within
`restricted_cash_share` (10%) instead. Two consecutive ties that break and cancel
are one balance sheet's cash out of line with the flows either side: one report,
on that balance sheet, keyed with the item (`p_error` 0.95). A tie that breaks
alone is reported on the cash flow (0.85).

**Quarter sums.** Each item holds within the larger of `identity_share` of its
largest value and `tie_floor`. The item that breaks most, against its
tolerance, names the row: the quarter whose value, less the gap, sits nearest
the median of the other three, or the year, where the quarters sit together and
the year is apart. `p_error` is 0.9 when that row is clear of the next by half a
unit, else 0.7. A year with two years in force (a change of fiscal year that
labels a transition period with the year it shares: the shape behind
rtrimble13/fafnir#217) is not judged, nor is a year with fewer than four
quarters. Every year gives one report at most, so no two share a key.

```
AAA  2010-06-30 Q2 FY2010 balance_sheet v1  DQ904 error  period-tie  cash_and_equivalents is out of line with the cash flows either side: off by 300000000, then by -300000000  (line 7)
BBB  2010-06-30 Q2 FY2010 cash_flow v1  DQ904 warn  period-tie  the change in cash_and_equivalents since 2010-03-31 is not net_change_in_cash: off by -200000000 (tolerance 24000000)  (line 10)
CCC  2010-09-30 Q3 FY2010 income v1  DQ904 error  period-tie  the quarters of FY2010 do not sum to the year: revenue by 1200000000; this quarter is out of line  (line 21)
```

**Keys.** A report's `record_key` is its row's version, with `tie` (`cash` or
`quarter_sum`), and for a balance sheet's cash also `item`:
`{"statement":"income","period_type":"quarter","fiscal_date":"2010-09-30","version_no":1,"tie":"quarter_sum"}`.
A cash report's detail holds `gap`, `tolerance` and `previous_fiscal_date` (or
`item` and `next_gap` for a balance sheet); a quarter sum's holds `fiscal_year`,
`items` (every item that breaks), `gap`, `tolerance` and `year_fiscal_date`.
`--since` keeps a report while any row of the tie was known or loaded on or
after the date.

**fafnir.** DQ904 succeeds `cash_balance_tie`, with its spacing and its
restricted-cash allowance from 2018. The quarter sums have no SQL predecessor:
`fundamentals_ytd_suspect` looks for year-to-date values stored as quarters,
which DQ913 judges (DR-0907).

## Fix

Re-fetch the row named. A balance sheet's cash out of line is a stored value
wrong; a cash flow's change is wrong, or the company's cash definition changed
(restricted cash before 2018, a held-for-sale reclassification). A quarter out of
line is a value wrong in that quarter, often year-to-date; a year out of line
with four quarters that sit together is the year's value wrong.
