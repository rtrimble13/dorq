# DQ904 period-tie

**Family:** statements, integrity and identities (DQ90x) · **Severity:** from
`p_error`; info for a filing fact · **Applies to:** statements · **Bayesian**
(DE-11) · **Needs:**
`[statements] cash_tie`, or `--items` for the quarter sums

## What it reports

A tie across periods that fails: what no identity within one row can see
([statements.md](../statements.md#identities-and-ties)). It reads the periods in
force (each period's open version).

| Tie | Judged | Reported on |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash.** The change in `cash_tie`'s first item between two consecutive balance sheets against the later period's change on the cash flow (the second item) | quarters `quarter_spacing` apart, and years `year_spacing` apart, with a cash flow for the later | the cash flow, or one balance sheet (below) |
| **Quarter sums.** A fiscal year's four quarters against its year, for each flow item `--items` lists on the income and cash flow statements | a fiscal year with exactly four quarters and one year in force, the quarters within the year | the quarter out of line, or the year |

**Cash.** The tie holds within the larger of `cash_tie_share` (2%) of its scale
and `tie_floor`. Its scale is the larger balance, or, where `--items` lists the
cash flow's flow items, the period's largest flow if that is larger (DE-11): a
reconciling difference (restricted cash, an exchange rate, cash held for sale)
scales with what flows through cash, not only with what is held. From
`restricted_cash_from` (2018-01-01; ASU 2016-18) the cash flow reconciles cash and
restricted cash, so it holds within `restricted_cash_share` (10%) instead. Two
consecutive ties that break and cancel (within the tolerance, and within 2% of
the gap) are one balance sheet's cash out of line with the flows either side: one
report, on that balance sheet, keyed with the item. A tie that breaks alone is
reported on the cash flow.

**Quarter sums.** Each item holds within the larger of `identity_share` of its
largest value and `tie_floor`. The item that breaks most, against its
tolerance, names the row: the quarter whose value, less the gap, sits nearest
the median of the other three, or the year, where the quarters sit together and
the year is apart; it is clear when its score is half a unit ahead of the next. A
year with two years in force (a change of fiscal year that
labels a transition period with the year it shares: the shape behind
rtrimble13/fafnir#217) is not judged, nor is a year with fewer than four
quarters. Every year gives one report at most, so no two share a key.

```
AAA  2010-06-30 Q2 FY2010 balance_sheet v1  DQ904 error  period-tie  cash_and_equivalents is out of line with the cash flows either side: off by 300000000, then by -300000000  (line 7)
BBB  2010-06-30 Q2 FY2010 cash_flow v1  DQ904 warn  period-tie  the change in cash_and_equivalents since 2010-03-31 is not net_change_in_cash: off by -200000000 (tolerance 24000000)  (line 10)
CCC  2010-09-30 Q3 FY2010 income v1  DQ904 error  period-tie  the quarters of FY2010 do not sum to the year: revenue by 1200000000; this quarter is out of line  (line 21)
```

**Keys.** A report's `record_key` is its row's version, with `tie` (`cash` or
`quarter_sum`), and for a balance sheet's cash also `item`:
`{"statement":"income","period_type":"quarter","fiscal_date":"2010-09-30","version_no":1,"tie":"quarter_sum"}`.
A cash report's detail holds `gap`, `tolerance` and `previous_fiscal_date` (or
`item` and `next_gap` for a balance sheet); a quarter sum's holds `fiscal_year`,
`items` (every item that breaks), `gap`, `tolerance` and `year_fiscal_date`.
`--since` keeps a report while any row of the tie was known or loaded on or
after the date.

**Why (DE-11, since 0.17.0).** A break is weighed as [DQ903](DQ903.md) weighs a
gap: a filing fact at the series' own rate for the tie (its breaks among its
other ties, of either period type for the cash tie, or other judged years for
the quarter sums, shrunk toward `[statements.break_rates]`' `cash` or
`quarter_sum`, else `break_rate`), structural where the series' other breaks
account for its gap, against a stored value wrong (`field_error`, or
`quarter_error` and `year_error` for a quarter sum). Evidence of the error: a
cancelling pair, or a clear quarter or year, each counted against how often the
series' other ties show one by chance; and a break where the series' ties hold.
So a lone break in a series whose cash has always tied is warn, and the same
break where the ties often break is info. `p_error` is the error's probability
(and `unexplained`'s with `unexplained_is_error`); before 0.17.0 it was fixed:
0.95 for a pair, 0.85 for a lone cash tie, 0.9 and 0.7 for a quarter sum with a
clear row and without.

A lone break that is no error says why: `; it breaks in 0 of 3 other quarters`,
or `; the series' own gap: it breaks in 19 of 40 other quarters`.

**A chronic tie.** A tie that breaks in at least `chronic_share` (75%) of at
least `chronic_min_periods` (8) periods of a type is reported once more for the
series, keyed on the statement (`cash_flow` for the cash tie, the flow statement
for its quarter sums), the period type and no period, with `tie`: at warn where
its median gap is at least `chronic_warn_share` (half) of the scale, else at
info ([DQ903](DQ903.md)).

**fafnir.** DQ904 succeeds `cash_balance_tie`, with its spacing and its
restricted-cash allowance from 2018. The quarter sums have no SQL predecessor:
`fundamentals_ytd_suspect` looks for year-to-date values stored as quarters,
which DQ913 judges (DR-0907).


**Score.** Each finding reports `detail.score` (DE-02): since 0.17.0 the model's
`p_error` before any map. With a map of DQ904's own (`dq904`), `p_error` is the
score through it; without one, through `dq90`. A `dq904` map fitted before 0.17.0
was fitted on the old score's scale, and needs fitting again. The detail also
holds `breaks`, `periods` and `break_rate`, as DQ903's, and a quarter sum's
`clear`.

## Fix

Re-fetch the row named. A balance sheet's cash out of line is a stored value
wrong; a cash flow's change is wrong, or the company's cash definition changed
(restricted cash before 2018, a held-for-sale reclassification). A quarter out of
line is a value wrong in that quarter, often year-to-date; a year out of line
with four quarters that sit together is the year's value wrong.
