# DQ302 sparse-series

**Family:** coverage · **Severity:** info · **Applies to:** daily series with at
least 60 sessions · **Classification:** market fact (`p_error` = 0)

## What it reports

A series that has a bar on fewer than `sparse_density` (default 80%) of the
sessions in its window. There is one violation per series, carrying the posterior
density and its 90% credible interval (Beta posterior, uniform prior).

```
MAIR  1998-01-02..2026-09-30  DQ302 info  sparse-series  a bar on 26.1% of 7,236 sessions (90% interval 25.3%-26.9%); a day without a bar is a fact about liquidity here, and DQ301 judges gaps against it
```

This is not a defect. It says that missing sessions in this series are normal,
and that DQ301 has taken that into account. It is the successor to fafnir's
`sparse_coverage` flag, without the per-session flags that cutoff replaced.

Info is hidden by default; `--show-info` shows it.

## Configuration

```toml
[coverage]
sparse_density = 0.8
```
