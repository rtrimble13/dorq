# DQ205 history-segment

**Family:** price action · **Severity:** by probability · **Applies to:** OHLCV
bars and positive point series · **Bayesian** (`p_error` = P(an error))

## What it reports

A long gap followed by a different level and regime: a new listing that
inherited a recycled ticker's history, such as a new ETF's first bar at about $25
or a SPAC's at $10 after years of another company's prices.

```
THN02  2018-06-01  DQ205 error  history-segment  after 250 sessions without a bar, close 4.26→25 (×5.869): another security's history may continue here; P(error) = >0.99
```

## The model

The `history_segment` hypothesis of the price model (doc/checks/DQ201.md),
considered only after `segment_gap` (60) sessions without a bar. Under it, the
log ratio of the two prices is that of two unrelated securities (normal, sd 1.2).
A market move over a long gap is allowed the volatility of all the sessions in
it. The evidence for a new history: a first close within 0.25% of 10, 20, 25, 50
or 100 (P = 0.3 for a new listing against 0.01), and a new volatility regime.

## Configuration

```toml
[price]
segment_gap = 60

[priors]
history_segment = 0.05
```

## Not reported

- Gaps shorter than `segment_gap` sessions. DQ301 reports the gap itself.

## Fix

End the old history at the gap and start a new series at the date given.
