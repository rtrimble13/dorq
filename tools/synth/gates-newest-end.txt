# dorq-synth's newest-bar universe (DE-13; `dorq-synth newest`), as of the night its
# events are on the last one to three bars: tests/synth_newest_gate.cmake. Same
# format as gates.txt, plus `<fault kind> recall`: the share of that kind's labels
# reported, at warn and above, under a code they expect.
#
# What is reported at warn and above is right. Until the bars after an event are
# in, its size alone cannot tell a bad print from a split, a change of scale or
# news, so 0.14, which reported each at warn, put a split's bars under DQ201 with
# a repair that deletes them (DQ201 precision 0.86-0.91 here; the 13 ETFs of
# 2026-11-19). They wait at info now.
DQ201 precision 0.95
DQ202 precision 0.95
DQ203 precision 0.95
DQ204 precision 0.95
# Real moves on the newest bar, by up to half or double, on 1.5 to 12 times the
# usual volume: never an error at warn.
newest_jump max 0
# Evidence in the bar itself (it moved on no volume, its close is outside its own
# range, or it is at a price no security trades at) settles a bad print at once.
newest_evident recall 0.9
